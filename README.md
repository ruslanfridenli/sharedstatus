# sharedstatus ☁️

a tiny, cozy ios widget for couples & close friends. 
no noise, no calls, no pressure — just gentle presence on your home screen.

### features
* **all lowercase aesthetic** — calm, minimal, and easy on the eyes.
* **wallpaper adaptive** — blends softly with your ios theme & tinted icons.
* **bilingual (de / en)** — switch between german and english in one tap.
* **zero tracking** — no emails, no passwords, just a simple 6-digit room code.

### stack
* `swift` & `swiftui`
* `widgetkit`
* `supabase` (coming next)
